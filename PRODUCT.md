# Lenchik Birthday Arcade

## Product Truth

Lenchik Birthday Arcade is a playful static birthday website for Lenya, made by a friend. It should feel like a personal retro console cartridge rather than a generic greeting card.

## Audience

The primary audience is Lenya and close friends who know the shared jokes: school days, the missing backpack, rowing, Formula 1, Counter-Strike, Moscow construction studies, and Eva.

## Experience

Visitors land in a pixel arcade menu, choose mini-games, earn points, and eventually unlock a birthday message. The site should work on GitHub Pages, desktop, and mobile without login or backend services.

## Required Games

- Hit Lenchik: whack-a-mole parody. Hit Lenya, avoid Eva with cake.
- Backpack Search: hidden-object school corridor game.
- Build Lenchik Tower: catch construction materials and avoid bad blueprints.
- Formula Lenya: top-down racing lane game.
- Row Lenchik Row: rhythm rowing challenge.
- Counter-Lenchik: aim trainer against harmless target objects.
- Match Lenchik: match-3 with Lenya-themed face tokens.
- Last Desk Escape: school maze inspired by Pac-Man.

## Characters

Lenya is a slim young man with short dark hair, thick eyebrows, expressive eyes, and a mix of serious and goofy expressions. Eva has dark hair and playful expressive poses. Both appear as pixel-art characters and small portraits. Eva is used in Hit Lenchik as the protected cake carrier.

## Constraints

- Static hosting on GitHub Pages.
- No backend.
- Fast load.
- Pixel-art style.
- Russian copy.
- Games must be playable, not just mockups.
